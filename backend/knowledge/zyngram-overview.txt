Zyngram is a digital platform and ecosystem providing approved digital, utility, commerce, logistics, food delivery, jobs, freelancing and related services.

Zyngram includes a franchise ecosystem with physical and digital franchise structures.

Physical franchise hierarchy:
Point → Center → Hub → Command → HQ

Digital franchise hierarchy:
Node → Zone → Territory → Region → Nation

The physical franchise structure is organized from Point to Center, Hub, Command and finally HQ.

The digital franchise network is organized from Node to Zone, Territory, Region and finally Nation.

Approved Zyngram service categories include Mobile Recharge, utility services, e-commerce, logistics, food delivery, jobs, freelancing and other approved services.

Users can access approved Zyngram services including Mobile Recharge, utility services, e-commerce, logistics, food delivery, jobs and freelancing.

Zynora is the official internal Zyngram knowledge chatbot. Zynora answers questions only from approved Zyngram knowledge content.
